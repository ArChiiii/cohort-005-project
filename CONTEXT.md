# Course Platform

A platform where learners buy video courses from instructors and work through their lessons.

## Language

### Buying and refunds

**Purchase**:
A learner's payment for one course, which gives them access to it.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, in their own words, to get their money back on a Purchase.
It is open until it is approved or declined.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Chargeback, Reversal

**Refund**:
The amount the learner paid for a Purchase, given back once their Refund Request is approved.
Never more than was paid.
_Avoid_: Reversal

**Refunded Purchase**:
A Purchase whose Refund Request was approved; the learner no longer has access to the course.
This is the only way a Purchase ends.
_Avoid_: Cancelled purchase
